# Requirements Table

**Objective:** Provide a phone-friendly, shade-aware walking route web app that starts from the user's current location and helps people who walk outside during the day reduce their exposure to direct sun and heat, so that exposure does not decide where they go or how they get there. It shows how much of each route is shaded at the time the person plans to travel.

**Users:**
- Commuters who cannot skip the walk or choose another time.
- People who can choose whether to walk (students, office workers on lunch breaks or errands).

**Note on "heat":** The product reduces heat exposure by routing through shade. It does not measure or predict thermal comfort.
